# Figure interpretations

**01_traffic_by_hour.png**: Weekdays show two clear rush hours: a morning peak at 07:00 (about 6,000 vehicles/hour) and a slightly higher evening peak at 16:00 (about 6,200). Weekends have no sharp peaks; traffic builds gradually to about 4,400 at midday and fades in the evening. Overnight traffic is very low on both, and weekend nights are slightly busier than weekday nights.

**02_traffic_distribution.png**: The distribution is not a single bell shape. There is a large spike of quiet overnight hours below about 1,000 vehicles, a middle group near 2,800 and a busy group around 4,500 to 5,000. The quartile lines show where the Low / Medium / High / Severe congestion categories begin (1,248 / 3,427 / 4,952), so Severe means roughly the busiest quarter of hours.

**03_traffic_by_weather.png**: Average traffic is highest in cloudy and hazy hours (about 3,600) and lowest in fog (about 2,700) and mist. Weather type is also tied to time of day (for example, fog is common at night), so this chart should not be read as weather causing the difference.

**04_temperature_vs_traffic.png**: There is no strong straight-line link between temperature and traffic (correlation about 0.14). Hours at every temperature range show the full spread from empty to very busy, so temperature alone is a weak predictor.

**05_congestion_heatmap.png**: Heavy traffic is concentrated on weekdays from about 06:00 to 18:00, with the darkest blocks at 07:00 and 15:00 to 17:00. Saturday and Sunday are lighter throughout, and every day is quiet between midnight and 04:00.
